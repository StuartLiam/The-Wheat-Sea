## Bruno's basic knowledge of his Familiar

Bruno would know the following information about his familiar based on his past reading and experimenting. Bruno's knowledge of his magic and summoning in general isn't complete and additional information can be learned with investigation.

- Bruno Performed a Ritual which his family showed him (how exactly TBD). This ritual summons the Deamon and binds it to the provided Relic mask, Making a Soulbound Devil.

- This Unlocks the Olorus Imp as a summonable familiar as per the find familiar spell, see statblock below

- When Bruno attempts to cast Find Familiar while having the Imp as his current Familiar. The attempt fails, the material components and spell slot are consumed and the spell does nothing.

- When the Devil is reduced to 0 HP it disappears and drops the mask at the location it disappeared from.

- Bruno may use the bound relic mask as the material component for the Find Familiar spell. When cast in this way, the Devil is resummoned to the mask and the exact same Devil becomes Bruno's familiar again. This method of casting find familiar supersedes the above general casting.

- If the mask is broken or either the caster or the devil decide to ritually sever the bond the devil immediately dies then reverts to a deamon and the caster permanently loses that portion of their soul.

- If a Devil familiar is dismissed, It may summon themself at any time.

- The Devils invisibility is not perfect. **In Ideal Circumstances** it is possible to see a shimmer and a distorted refraction off of the devil as they are invisible.

- under ideal vision circumstances, while the devil is moving detection is roughly as follows. 

-- within 5ft of the Devil: detetection is automatic
-- from 10ft to 60ft: DC 10 Search Action.
-- from 60ft to 120ft: DC equal to Spell Casting DC Search Action.
-- from 120ft+: Basically Impossible

- if the devil is standing still it is one level harder to spot. 

- terrain and conditions that favor hiding will make the devil harder to spot

- Animals and creatures that don't rely solely on vision have a much easier time detecting the Imp. Dogs for example seem to notice it very easily.

- The Imp is always visable to creatures and features capable of detecting magic.

- Magical glyphs set to triggen on interaction or detection of a creature will trigger on the imp even if it is invisable.

- The Devil is knowledgeable and can learn, but does not have memories from before its creation.

- The Devil cannot be commanded to perform an action it is not willing to do. Which is most things.

- The Devil can be Bribed with Luxuries and common vices. It isn't particulary picky, but understands bargining power and will use it.

- The Devil has a self preservation drive and does not want to take damage.

# Olorus Imp
*Tiny Fiend (Devil) — Soulbound Familiar*

---

**Armor Class** 13
**Hit Points** 10 (3d4+3)
**Speed** 20 ft., climb 20 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 6 (−2) | 17 (+3) | 13 (+1) | 13 (+1) | 7 (-2) | 14 (+2) |

**Skills** Insight +0, Persuasion +4, Stealth +5, Perception +0;
**Damage Resistances** cold; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical weapons that aren't silvered
**Senses** darkvision 30 ft. (see in magical darkness), Passive Perception 11
**Languages** Cerik, Dead Eema (Demonic); understands its master's languages
**Challenge** 1
**Morale** Self-preserving — will not knowingly risk death (see notes above)

---

***Magic Resistance.*** The imp has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Devil's Sight.*** Magical darkness doesn't impede the imp's darkvision.

***GM Carte Blanche*** The Imp is not forthwrite with the full breadth of its abilities the GM may at any point reveal the imps hidden abilities.

***Spider Climb.*** The imp can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

***Soulbound.*** The imp is bound to its relic mask. At 0 HP it disappears and drops the mask where it fell; it can be resummoned via Find Familiar using the mask as the material component. Severing the bond or breaking the mask kills the devil permanently. See notes above for full binding rules.

---

### Actions

***Claw.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d4+3) slashing damage.

***Invisibility (Imperfect).*** The imp magically turns invisible until it attacks, or until its concentration ends (as if concentrating on a spell). Its invisibility is imperfect — detection follows the distance, movement, and sensory rules in Bruno's notes. It is always visible to magic detection, triggers interaction/detection glyphs, and equipment/effects carried remain visible.
