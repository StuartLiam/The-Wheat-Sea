## A Knight's Duties

The term knight in the context of the empire refers to 2 subcategory of professional fighter.

- A Knight Heraldic, these knights are the sons and daughters of landed nobles. From the age of 6-8 children of wealthy houses apprentice as squires under knights. They do menial work and learn the traditions of knighthood until their early 20s, at which point they are usually knighted as a full warrior.

- A Knight Banneret, these knights are any professional fighter, mercenary or man at arms, with sufficient skill and resources to be picked up by a lord as a retained knight. They are allowed a last name and allowed to carry their own banner into battle.

Astrid, as a layperson would be the latter. Originally a soldier/man at arms that was elevated to knighthood.

Sworn to the service of a lord, a knight is basically the local military, Police, Firefighter and general work force of the lord.

### A knight would be expected to do any of the following duties.

- Tax collector, would work with a Bureaucrat minor noble to collect the field tax from the provinces villages in the lords domain, this is collected twice a year, after the first harvest and after the third harvest. this tax is usually one third of all harvested items.

- Raise a Levy. In times of war your lord would be required by their Dome to levy a force of fighters from the citizens. groups of knights would go village to village raising the army. Depending on the requirement this percentage varies but a typical levy raises 1 fighter per 5 hearths, or equivalent pay.

- Construction and repair, knights are often used to maintain provincial infrastructure, like aqueducts, roads and walls. This is a year round task but especially during the time of storms, deterring and repairing flood and wind damage. 

- Competitor, Tournaments and competitions are consistent sources of entertainment and community. knights were expected and incentivised to participate in these displays of skill. Jousts and carousels were usually reserved for Heraldic knights but larger tournaments usually had secondary leagues for bannerets. All tournaments had duels and melees that bannerets could enter.

- During the first planting, A knight could be expected to work the lords fields and during all harvests a knight could be expected to harvest the lords fields.

- Warden, There are many large and terrible beasts in the wilds and on the outskirts of towns, for particularly dangerous beasts, knights were often called on to slay the beast and harvest its components. This was usually the most desirable job beside jousting, as it garnered a knight the most status.

### Wartime knowledge and duties

- During wartime a knight may take on a number of roles in the army, most often as cavalry but also as field captains, scouts and cavaliers.

- As a field captain Astrid would be expected to fight with up to 4 man at arms. these would be other professional soldiers.

- As a field captain Astrid would be responsible for up to 4 groups of 12 fighters. these fighters would be levied citizens that brought their own fighting gear. Usually this would be making sure they didn't rout, received orders and teach them basic formations. Can depend on the conflict but a captain would often rotate through these groups, often fighting with them for only one winter.

- Astrid could have up to 3 squires

- Most knights cannot read, as a caster Astrid is kinda 50/50 on if she can read, I'll leave that up to you. but generally knights don't see reading and writing as useful. this sentiment is more common in bannerets, as heraldic knights have more social obligations.

- You wouldn't have your own estate unless you were an older knight. You, your squires and your men at arms would have quarters in a house at arms, barracks or fortress. this would usually be 3-4 rooms, one for you, one for your squires and one for your men at arms.

- Depending on their salary and schedule the men at arms would sometimes take second jobs, especially for the poorer bannerets

- A knight is expected to maintain 1 - 2 Qural's (horse creature) of good breed for yourself, your man at arms maintain their own if they have enough coin.

### a knights knowledge and wheelhouse

- A knight is expected to be religious and follow the olorus faith. As a follower you have daily ceremony obligations but usually just participation.

- A knight would know the basics of courtly manner

- A knight would know the main heraldry by banner and name, and would be able to identify more minor houses some of the time.

- A knight would know how the empire works at a governmental level, what the houses do, what the domes are, what the council of wings is, who the triamo is and how it all works together.

- A knight would understand the logistics and strategy of warfare.
